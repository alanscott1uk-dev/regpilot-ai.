# Universal compliance platform architecture

RegPilot AI aims to serve multiple sectors through a shared compliance engine and configurable industry-specific modules.

## Shared core
Regulatory sources, obligation registers, licence/certificate tracking, policies, audits, risk, health and safety, HR, data protection, training and management reporting.

## Industry-specific modules
Supply chain, imports, environmental management and specialist sector workflows are enabled according to each business's actual operations.

## Prototype
- Industry and department templates
- Configurable module catalogue with sector-based recommendations
- Browser-local settings only
- No automated determination that a business must comply with a given regulation

## Production design
Every company has its own organisation ID, departments, users, role permissions, modules and evidence. Each obligation must record jurisdiction, regulatory source, applicability rationale, owner, effective date, review date, and approval history. Regulatory changes must be independently reviewed before they become assigned obligations.

A module recommendation is a UI suggestion, not legal advice. Real compliance coverage requires verified sources, specialist review and jurisdiction-aware applicability rules.
