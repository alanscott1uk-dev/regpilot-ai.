# RegPilot UK regulatory coverage — design register

The initial authority catalogue includes 20 official bodies spanning healthcare, controlled drugs, company administration, data protection, tax, food, environment, transport, consumer products, communications and employment guidance.

Authority records include ID, official source, jurisdiction, sector, and authority type. Matching produces **candidate authorities only**. A company must not be described as regulated by a body merely because its industry matches.

## Jurisdiction handling
- UK: potentially relevant throughout the UK.
- GB: England, Scotland and Wales only.
- Devolved: England, Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland as specifically tagged.
- Specific requirements may have narrower application than the authority's jurisdiction; every future rule needs its own jurisdiction and applicability metadata.

## Before activating regulatory obligations
1. Confirm each official URL resolves to the intended authority.
2. Add authoritative legislation and guidance records with versions and checked dates.
3. Verify whether the business's activities trigger a requirement and whether exemptions apply.
4. Require sector-expert approval of the rule interpretation.
5. Monitor changes to source documents and route changes to human review.
6. Publish source-backed, auditable obligations only after validation.

The authority catalogue is not a regulatory endorsement, accreditation, compliance score, or legal opinion.
