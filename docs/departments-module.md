# Cross-industry departments

Route: `/departments`

RegPilot AI is intended to support multiple sectors. This module introduces ten optional industry templates and editable department lists. Templates are organisational suggestions, not regulatory requirements.

## Current prototype
- Fictional company profile and industry selector
- Suggested department templates for healthcare, manufacturing, food, logistics, construction, financial services, retail, technology, hospitality and general businesses
- Add/remove custom departments
- Browser-local configuration with basic validation

## Future integration
- Persist organisation and department records in PostgreSQL
- Link obligations, evidence, policies, incidents and audits to departments
- Assign users, department managers and reviewers
- Apply department-specific access controls
- Add sector-specific regulatory source coverage and verified obligation templates
- Provide company-wide and department-level reporting

No actual user accounts or cross-device sharing are active. Do not enter confidential company details in this demonstration.
